# Redstonewall Jackson

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.248 / Java 21. Mod id `redstonewalljackson`, package
`com.chunkworks.redstonewalljackson`, repo `minecraft-redstonewall-jackson`. AGPL-3.0-or-later,
Rusty Shackleford and nfx, to the MIT 6.031 bar.

## What this is

Redstone dust, repeaters and comparators on walls, behaving exactly as on a floor. The
same items place them (click a wall face), they look the same (vanilla's own textures and
colour), they drop the same items, and their rules are vanilla's, written once against a
plane and stood up. Rusty asked for it on 2026-09-23 and named it.

## Shape

- `src/domain`, JDK only: `Dir`, `Planar`, `Frame` (a plane as an orientation of the floor),
  `Cell`/`View` (a wire's neighbourhood, turned into its frame), `Shape`/`Joint`, `Wire`
  (vanilla's dust rules over a view) and `Diode` (a diode's sides and placement facing).
  15 JUnit tests with partitions at the top of each class.
- `src/main`: `WallRedstoneWireBlock`, `WallDiodeBlock` with `WallRepeaterBlock` and
  `WallComparatorBlock` (vanilla's diodes ported through the frame), `Views` (reads the
  level into a `View`), `Frames` (directions and plane-coordinate boxes), `Placement` (the
  vanilla items place the wall forms), one client colour handler, and one mixin so
  vanilla's floor dust reads wall dust's power at a seam.
- `src/gametest`: real-server tests of a wall run against its floor twin, power falloff,
  the three seams, repeater delay and lock, comparator compare and subtract from a chest,
  breaking, placement by click and pick-block; and the photo booth.
- Models and blockstates are generated by `devtools/models/generate.py` from vanilla's
  resources and committed.

Decisions: [D-0001](decisions/D-0001.md).

## Status

0.1.0 built 2026-09-23: 15 JUnit, 11 real-server GameTests, the booth's five photos on the
GPU inspected (`devtools/verification/0.1.0.md`). Unreleased, no remote, until Rusty's go.
Ceilings are not in scope; the frame admits them in one line when asked. A wall repeater
cannot lock a floor repeater beside it (vanilla's lock check is a class check that cannot be
widened safely); everything else crosses the seam.
