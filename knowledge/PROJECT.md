# Redstonewall Jackson

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.248 / Java 21. Mod id `redstonewalljackson`, package
`com.chunkworks.redstonewalljackson`, repo `minecraft-redstonewall-jackson`. AGPL-3.0-or-later,
Rusty Shackleford and nfx, to the MIT 6.031 bar.

## What this is

Redstone dust, repeaters and comparators on walls and on the undersides of blocks, behaving
exactly as on a floor. The same items place them (click a wall face or a block's underside),
they look the same (vanilla's own textures and colour), they drop the same items, and their
rules are vanilla's, written once against a plane and turned. Every corner between planes
draws and carries power. Rusty asked for it on 2026-09-23 and named it; the corners and the
undersides came the same evening.

## Shape

- `src/domain`, JDK only: `Dir`, `Planar`, `Frame` (a plane as an orientation of the floor:
  the floor, four walls, the ceiling), `Cell`/`View` (a wire's neighbourhood, turned into its
  frame, with which side the wire in front rests on), `Shape`/`Joint`, `Wire` (vanilla's dust
  rules over a view, plus the inside corner) and `Diode` (a diode's sides and placement
  facing). 16 JUnit tests with partitions at the top of each class.
- `src/main`: `WallRedstoneWireBlock`, `WallDiodeBlock` with `WallRepeaterBlock` and
  `WallComparatorBlock` (vanilla's diodes ported through the frame; `FACING`/`WALL` is the
  plane's normal, a horizontal or down), `Views` (reads the level into a `View`), `Frames`
  (directions and plane-coordinate boxes), `Placement` (the vanilla items place the wall and
  ceiling forms), one client colour handler, and one mixin class: floor dust reads wall dust's
  power at the wall-top seam and directly above it, draws its joint toward a wall run above as
  the climb, and shares vanilla's wire-silence flag.
- `src/gametest`: real-server tests in three classes, walls (`WallGameTests`), corners
  (`CornerGameTests`) and ceilings (`CeilingGameTests`), over a shared `Rig`; and the photo
  booth with the wall, the pillar, the stair (floor, wall, ceiling, round the edge) and the
  two-wall corner.
- Models and blockstates are generated by `devtools/models/generate.py` from vanilla's
  resources and committed; the ceiling is the north-wall models under one x rotation.

Decisions: [D-0001](decisions/D-0001.md), [D-0002](decisions/D-0002.md).

## Status

0.2.0 built 2026-09-23 (evening): corners drawn as vanilla's climb, ceilings, and vanilla
floor dust joining a wall run above it. 16 JUnit, 24 real-server GameTests, the booth's
thirteen photos on the GPU inspected (`devtools/verification/0.2.0.md`). Not yet released;
Rusty's go is next, together with the Backpacks+ fixes asked for the same evening.

0.1.0 (2026-09-23, pack 1.57.1) worked at every corner but drew none of the inside ones:
Rusty's "seems like wall redstone does not corner". A wall repeater cannot lock a floor
repeater beside it (vanilla's lock check is a class check that cannot be widened safely);
everything else crosses every seam.
