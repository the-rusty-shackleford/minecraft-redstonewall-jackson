# 0.1.0 verification — 2026-09-23

Rusty: "make redstone dust, repeaters, etc applicable to walls. They should present and work
exactly as if they were on a floor, but its a wall." Design D-0001, previewed and acked
("alright proceed your way").

- `./gradlew --offline --no-watch-fs test`: 15 JUnit tests against the JDK-only domain.
  `FrameTest` (the floor is vanilla's map; a north wall has up at the top and, standing
  before it, east on the left; every wall's planar directions are the four perpendicular to
  its normal, each once, and the inverse agrees; ceilings and vertical normals refused;
  directions turn as the game's do). `WireTest` (joints from what stands beside, including
  the climb, the non-sturdy face, the conductor in front and the step down; alone is a
  cross unless it was a dot; lines, corners, tees, crosses; one side changing; the click;
  power as the strongest signal or one less than the strongest wire touched, with the
  climb, the step, the seam in front and the strongest of everything; signals into the
  support and along connected sides in every frame, nothing to wires or forward; invariants).
  `DiodeTest` (sides beside the facing on the floor and on a wall; placement facing from
  the look, ties and the ignored normal component).
- `./gradlew --offline --no-watch-fs runGameTestServer`: all 11 required real-server
  GameTests passed. A wall run against the floor run it is a turned copy of, power and all
  four joints block by block, both lamps lit; power falling by one a step up ten blocks of
  wall; the seam up from a floor run in front of the wall, down from the wall onto a floor
  run, and from the wall onto a floor run along its top edge (through the mixin); a wall
  repeater passing full power after two and eight ticks; a wall repeater locked by a
  sideways wall repeater and passing nothing while its input is powered; a wall comparator
  reading a chest below it along the wall (ten) against a side comparator reading its own
  chest (four): ten in compare mode, six in subtract; the wall block removed takes the dust
  and drops redstone; a mock server player in survival clicking a wall face with dust, a
  repeater and a comparator places the three wall forms, the repeater's output where the
  player looks, one item spent each, and clicking a floor places vanilla's dust; the wall
  forms pick as the vanilla items.
  The first run found the mod's real defect: vanilla silences all its wires through a
  private flag while any wire computes, and without sharing it the wire above a comparator
  read its own power back through the wall block and stuck, and the wall-top seam leaked
  full strength both ways. Fixed with an accessor onto that flag, set and honoured both
  ways.
