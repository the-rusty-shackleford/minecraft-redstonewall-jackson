# 0.2.0 verification — 2026-09-23 (evening)

Rusty, with 0.1.0 on the box: "seems like wall redstone does not corner", "Just test em
both", then "Oh it should also work on the underside of blocks, and corner upwards/downwards
as well." Design D-0002.

What 0.1.0 actually did: every corner carried power (the L on one wall and both two-wall
corners passed on a real server before any change, and the L photographed right on all four
walls, `0.2.0/corner-*.png`), but no inside corner was *drawn*: in 0.1.0's own seam photo the
floor line stops a block short of the wall and the wall line starts at the floor with nothing
between. 0.2.0 draws every inside corner as vanilla's climb and adds ceilings.

- `./gradlew --offline --no-watch-fs test`: 16 JUnit tests against the JDK-only domain
  (`FrameTest` 6: the floor; a north wall; the ceiling as the north wall tipped back, with the
  left hand's way unchanged; every frame's planar directions perpendicular to its normal, each
  once, with the inverse agreeing; vertical wall normals refused; directions turn as the game's
  do. `WireTest` 8: joints now including the inside corner, a climb onto the block beside that
  the wire in front rests on, SIDE for a face that is not sturdy or an overhang, nothing toward
  the other sides, and no corner for a wire in front resting on nothing beside; the corner
  shape; power counting a wire in front only when it rests on a side and never one facing this
  wire across a gap; signals in every frame including the ceiling; the view's new invariant.
  `DiodeTest` 2: the ceiling's placement facing and sides).
- `./gradlew --offline --no-watch-fs runGameTestServer`: all 24 required real-server
  GameTests passed, first run. The eleven of 0.1.0; `CornerGameTests` (7): the L on one wall
  in both placement orders; a floor run up a wall from the dust at its foot (12 at the foot,
  11 above, 9 at the top; the floor dust's joint toward the wall is UP, vanilla's climb, through
  the new inject) and a wall run down onto it (15 at the top beside the source, 12 at the foot
  through the second inject, 9 along the floor); the inside corner between two walls with the
  corner cell on either wall (the corner cell's joint toward the other wall UP, power 11 → 10 →
  9 → 6); the outside corner round a pillar. `CeilingGameTests` (6): a ceiling run against its
  floor twin, power and all four joints block by block, both lamps lit; a wall run up onto the
  ceiling with the ceiling run in front of the top wall dust (TOP is UP) and with it directly
  above the wall run (the ceiling dust's BOTTOM is UP), 13 → 12 and 14 → 13; the same corner
  down from the ceiling, 12 → 11 and 11 → 10; a ceiling run round the edge of the block it
  hangs from to a lamp; a ceiling repeater passing full power after two and eight ticks; a
  mock survival player clicking the underside of a block with dust, a repeater and a comparator
  placing the three ceiling forms (the repeater's output where the player looks), one item
  spent each.
